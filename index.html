The Learning Cycle

How LearnFlex Personalises Every Session

A closed-loop adaptive cycle that turns diagnostic assessments into explainable learning breakthroughs.

1

Baseline Diagnostic

New learners receive a balanced 3-question diagnostic across Foundation, Standard, and Challenge tiers to establish baseline ability.

2

Dual-Signal Capture

Students submit their chosen answer along with self-reported confidence (High, Medium, Low), distinguishing guesses from true mastery.

3

Adaptive Routing

Confident errors route immediately to Foundation review; uncertain answers route to Reinforcement; repeated success unlocks Challenge items.

4

Misconception Intervention

Identifies the underlying mathematical or conceptual flaw, models a worked example, and tests a fresh follow-up verification question.

5

Real-Data Persistence

Attempts are validated on the server, saved to persistent SQLite storage, and aggregated for class-scoped teacher dashboards.

Explainable Intelligence

The 4-Tier Adaptive Framework Explained

Every adaptive decision made by LearnFlex is explainable, transparent, and pedagogically justified.

Tier 1 · Foundation

Prerequisite Scaffolding

Focuses on core definitions, visual fraction bars, number lines, and step-by-step prerequisite ideas when rolling accuracy falls below 60% or confident mistakes occur.

Example Focus: In 3/5, visualising that 5 is the number of equal parts and 3 is the count of parts taken.
Tier 2 · Reinforcement

Guided Parallel Practice

Delivered when a student answers correctly but reports low confidence, or after recovering from an error. Uses fresh parallel examples with structured support.

Example Focus: Solving 2/7 + 3/7 with common denominator guidance before introducing unlike fractions.
Tier 3 · Standard

Grade-Level Benchmark

The standard NCERT textbook curriculum level. Tests standard problem-solving procedures, chapter exercises, and typical CBSE examination patterns.

Example Focus: Simplifying complex fractions and converting mixed fractions into improper fractions.
Tier 4 · Challenge

High-Order Thinking (HOTS)

Unlocked when students achieve ≥80% accuracy with consistent high confidence. Involves multi-step word problems, data interpretation, and real-world modelling.

Example Focus: Multi-step rate problems dividing unequal parts across varied units.

Data Protection

Privacy Policy & Child Safety Statement

Privacy and child-safety overview for this demonstration. A school/legal review is required before real student deployment.

1. Purpose & Scope

LearnFlex is dedicated to educational formative assessment for CBSE students in Classes 6 to 10. We process only minimal, necessary learning performance information required to calculate question difficulty and generate pedagogical recommendations.

2. Collection of Student Data

The platform stores student roll numbers/IDs, class designations, question attempts, answer selections, self-reported confidence, and timestamps. We do not collect government identity cards, biometric data, precise GPS coordinates, contact phone numbers, or financial information.

3. Local-First Storage & Offline Priority

All learning history is initially recorded in the learner's own browser storage (IndexedDB and localStorage). When synchronizing with the backend server, data is transmitted over encrypted TLS connections directly to the designated school server.

4. Role-Scoped Teacher Authorization

A teacher is authorized to access assessment records *only* for students in their assigned class and school. Cross-class and cross-school student data inspection is prohibited and blocked by server-side scoping algorithms.

5. Right to Erasure & Deletion

The student profile provides a DELETE /api/account endpoint to remove the account and associated learning records held by this demo backend. Retained security logs are redacted; browser copies on other devices and backups may require separate handling. This is an application feature description, not legal advice or a certification of statutory compliance.
